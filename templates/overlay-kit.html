How to turn this into footage

    Record — OBS window/browser-source capture, cropped to one frame at 1080×1920. 6–8s covers a full cycle.

  • 02

    Trim — cut to one clean cycle in CapCut and save it as a reusable overlay clip.

  • 03

    Layer — drop it on a track above your footage, mute its audio.
